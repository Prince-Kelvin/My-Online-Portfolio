About Me

I'm Prince Kelvin Samuel, a Business Analyst with experience
in business process improvement, requirements analysis, stakeholder engagement, and data-driven decision-making.

I work at the intersection of business needs, technology, and data, helping translate business challenges into clear requirements and practical solutions. My experience spans banking and finance, healthcare, logistics, and supply chain management, with exposure to both Agile and Waterfall methodologies.

My core competencies include requirements gathering and documentation, user stories and acceptance criteria, backlog grooming, sprint planning, process mapping, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and stakeholder coordination. I also leverage SQL, Microsoft Excel, and Power BI to analyse data, develop dashboards, and support evidence-based decisions.

I am passionate about improving processes, bridging communication between business and technical teams, and delivering solutions that create measurable business value. Outside work, I enjoy football, movies, and reading.
